Borsat Dinari

An application that provides exchange rates for the US Dollar against the Iraqi Dinar across most governorates of Iraq and the Kurdistan Region, as well as global currency rates relative to the Iraqi Dinar, updated every 15 minutes.

App Features:

Free for life

Ad-free for life

Non-profit for life

Requires no permissions

Free from annoying notifications

Very lightweight and fast

Does not collect your personal data

Keeps a price history record for the last 60 days

Terms of Use:
There are no copyrights or distribution restrictions; you are free to use the app in any manner.

Important Disclaimer:
Currency rates are for informational and educational purposes only. The application assumes no responsibility for user financial decisions during trading or exchange.

A widget is available that you can add from your phone's home screen to display your preferred exchange rates, featuring an automatic update every 30 minutes alongside an instant refresh button.

Color Guide:

Red: Price drop

Green: Price rise

Gray: Price stable or no update available currently
