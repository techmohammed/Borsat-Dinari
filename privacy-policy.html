Privacy Policy

Borsat Dinari

Effective date: November 6, 2026

1. Introduction

Borsat Dinari is an independent open-source project that provides Iraqi currency exchange rates, international currency information, precious metals prices, and financial market updates.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when we use third-party services and APIs to manage and publish content on our official social media accounts.

2. Information We Access and Process

When connecting our official Pinterest Business account to the Pinterest API, we may access and process information necessary to operate the integration, including:

  • Our authorized Pinterest account identifier.
  • Our own Pins and Boards and their relevant metadata.
  • Images, titles, descriptions, and links submitted for publishing.
  • API responses and publishing status information.

We use this information only to manage and publish content to our own authorized Pinterest account. We do not use this integration to access or manage other Pinterest users' accounts.

3. Pinterest API Usage

The Pinterest API is used to support content publishing and management for the official Borsat Dinari account. Content may include financial information, exchange-rate updates, informational images, and project announcements.

We do not sell Pinterest data or use it for advertising profiling. We do not collect Pinterest users' private information through this integration.

4. Storage and Security

API access tokens and credentials used by our automated publishing workflows are stored as encrypted repository secrets on GitHub Actions.

These credentials are used only by authorized publishing workflows. We take reasonable measures to prevent unauthorized access and avoid exposing credentials in workflow logs.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information and content submitted through the Pinterest API are processed by Pinterest according to its own policies and terms.

Our source code and automated workflows are hosted and executed using GitHub services, which are subject to GitHub's own privacy policy.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only as needed to operate and maintain our publishing integration. We do not intend to maintain a separate database of personal information belonging to Pinterest users.

Access to Pinterest can be revoked through the user's Pinterest account settings. We can also remove stored API credentials from our GitHub repository secrets when the integration is discontinued.

Content already published on Pinterest may remain on Pinterest until it is deleted through the Pinterest account.

7. Third-Party Services

This project may use the following services:

  • Pinterest Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Facebook Privacy Policy
  • Instagram Privacy Policy

8. Open-Source Project

Borsat Dinari is an open-source project. Its source code is publicly available at: Borsat Dinari on GitHub .

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or data practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For questions or requests concerning this Privacy Policy, contact us through our official Borsat Dinari Telegram channel .
